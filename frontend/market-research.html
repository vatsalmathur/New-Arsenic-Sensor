1. Economic Model & Projections

Our revenue model utilizes a hybrid Hardware-Enabled SaaS approach. We generate upfront capital through the sale of the autonomous screening robots, and build long-term recurring revenue through a monthly data platform subscription (access to live mapping, alerts, and historical contour analytics).

Monthly Revenue Formula:

Rt = (Ut × Pu) + (St × Ps)

Where:
Rt = Total Gross Revenue for month t
Ut = Units sold in month t
Pu = Price per robot unit (Est. $3,500)
St = Active dashboard subscriptions in month t
Ps = Subscription fee per month (Est. $150/mo)

First Month (Pilot Launch)

  • Target: Local water authorities, NGOs.
  • Hardware: 5 units × $3,500 = $17,500
  • Software: 5 users × $150 = $750
  • Month 1 Gross: $18,250

Year 1 (Scaling Phase)

  • Target: Municipal water boards globally.
  • Hardware: 120 units sold = $420,000
  • Software: Avg 60 active × 12mo × $150 = $108,000
  • Year 1 Gross: $528,000

3. Market Research & Economic Trend Predictions

The global demand for decentralized, rapid water quality testing is accelerating due to environmental pressures and aging infrastructure.

Target Market Size

The Global Water Quality Monitoring market is projected to reach $7.5 Billion by 2028, growing at a CAGR of roughly 6.5%. Specifically, the segment for heavy metals (like Arsenic) and environmental surface water testing represents a highly underserved ~$1.2 Billion niche.

Economic Trends Driving Adoption

  • Heightened Regulatory Pressure: Governments are lowering permissible arsenic limits (WHO standard is strictly 10 µg/L).
  • The Cost of Human Labor: Our robotic model slashes the per-sample acquisition cost by over 90% compared to technicians in boats.
  • Climate Change Anomalies: Shifting water tables and severe droughts are causing groundwater arsenic spikes globally.

AquaSentry Market Prediction

By positioning AquaSentry as a highly affordable "screening tool", we do not compete with million-dollar certified lab instruments. Instead, we become the mandatory first step. We predict capturing 5% of the early-stage environmental screening market within our first 36 months of full-scale production, establishing a powerful barrier to entry through our accumulated, proprietary water quality dataset.
